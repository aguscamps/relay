Questions, answered.

Who is Relay built for?

Procurement and Operations teams at large organizations, with many vendors, teams and processes across different countries.

Will it work with the systems we already use?

Yes. Relay integrates with the systems your teams already use, including SAP, Salesforce and Microsoft Dynamics. Using a different system? Ask our team during your demo and we'll look at how to connect it.

Can it adapt to our approval process?

Yes. Approval workflows are configurable, so they follow your organization's structure and requirements.

How does Relay use AI?

It flags potential compliance and risk issues, such as an expired insurance certificate or missing tax registration, so your team can review them early.

Does it work with other Relay solutions?

Yes. Vendor Management is available in Relay and works alongside any other Relay solutions your organization already uses.

How long does it take to get started?

Relay adapts to the processes and tools your teams already use, so nobody has to change everything at once. Teams keep working in their current systems while vendors move over. In your demo, we'll walk you through what setting it up would look like for your organization.

How much does Relay cost?

Our sales team will walk you through pricing during your demo, based on your organization's needs.

What happens after I request a demo?

Our sales team reviews your request, contacts you and arranges a demo.
